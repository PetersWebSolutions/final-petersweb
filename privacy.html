Privacy Policy

How PetersWeb Solutions handles the information you share through this website.

Last updated: October 20, 2026 Applies to: peterswebsolutions.com, the inquiry form, and project records

1. Who we are

PetersWeb Solutions (“PetersWeb”, “we”, “us”) provides website development and business systems for small businesses. We are based at Windsor Tower, Makati, Philippines.

2. Information you provide

When you submit the inquiry form on this website, we collect the details you enter, which may include:

  • Your full name
  • Your business name
  • Business type
  • Package interest
  • Current website address, if you choose to provide one
  • Your email address and mobile number, both required for inquiry follow-up
  • A short description of your project needs

When an inquiry is submitted, we store a reference number and the page path from which it was sent. If an inquiry becomes a project, we may also record the agreed package, agreed project price, add-ons, scheduled and received payment amounts, payment type/status and due or received dates, project stage, domain name and provider, domain renewal date, the optional email address used for the domain, and internal project notes. The dashboard can show an in-app reminder when a recorded domain renewal date is within 30 days or overdue. It does not send email or SMS reminders. Please do not submit or store passwords, payment-card or bank credentials, or email, hosting, or domain-registrar passwords in the form or admin dashboard.

3. How we use your information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your inquiry and clarify your requirements
  • Prepare a quotation or recommend a website package
  • Communicate with you about a potential or active project
  • Keep a record of inquiries and, where a project proceeds, manage its stage, agreed scope, domain provider and renewal details, and manually recorded payment schedules and receipts

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use inquiry details for unrelated marketing messages without your consent.

4. Storage and retention

Inquiry and project records are stored in a Supabase-managed database, while the website and its API are hosted on Vercel. Access to the admin dashboard is restricted to the authorized admin account. We keep information only for as long as needed for the purposes described above, for legitimate business records, or as required by applicable law. The dashboard records payment amounts for bookkeeping; it does not process payments or store card or bank credentials.

5. Cookies and analytics

This website does not load an analytics provider or set advertising or analytics cookies by default. It exposes a custom conversion event for a future integration; that event can contain only a generic event name and, for package-related actions, a package code. It never includes names, contact details, website addresses, or free-text inquiry content. The Google Map is not loaded until you choose to load the interactive map. Embedded portfolio websites, Messenger, Google Maps, and other external services you choose to open are operated by third parties and governed by their own privacy policies.

6. Sharing and third-party services

We may share information with service providers that help us operate our website, hosting, communications, or business administration, strictly for those purposes and subject to appropriate safeguards. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect our legal rights.

7. Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, including the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173), you may ask to access, correct, or delete the personal information you submitted, or ask us to stop contacting you. To make a request, contact us through the inquiry form on the main website or by writing to us at Windsor Tower, Makati, Philippines. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

If you believe your privacy rights have been violated, you may contact the National Privacy Commission of the Philippines or the relevant authority in your jurisdiction.

8. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information submitted to us. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above shows the most recent revision. Continued use of this website after an update means the revised policy applies.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the inquiry form on our contact section or write to PetersWeb Solutions, Windsor Tower, Makati, Philippines.
