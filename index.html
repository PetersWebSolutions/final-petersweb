Questions, answered

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to the details business owners usually ask about before starting a website project.

Still unsure about something?

Send us your question
What is included in each website package?

Basic, Pro, and Business include the page ranges, first-year domain and hosting, revision period, support, and features shown in the package comparison. Basic has a separate ₱499/month subscription for six months. Pro is ₱8,999 one-time, and Business is ₱14,999 one-time. Booking/dashboard scope, support limits, subscription terms, and any conditional follower add-on are specified in writing before purchase.

I already have a Facebook page. Why add a website?

Your Facebook page can keep serving your followers and community. A website complements it with one organized place for your services, location, hours, contact details, and booking information, and gives you a destination to share across channels. It does not replace Facebook or guarantee more customers.

My business is small. Do I need a website?

A website is optional, but can give a small business a clear home for key information and inquiries. Basic is designed for a smaller 1–3 page scope; we can also help you decide whether a website fits your needs before you commit. No sales or booking results are guaranteed.

I don’t know what content to put on a website. Can you help?

Start with the essentials: what you offer, who you serve, location and hours, photos you have permission to use, and how customers should contact or book. We can help outline the page structure. You provide accurate business details and approved materials; copywriting or other content work will be confirmed in the project scope.

Is the domain and hosting included?

Domain and hosting for the first year are included in each listed package. Renewal costs and arrangements after the first year must be confirmed before purchase; they are not implied to be free or included beyond year one.

What happens after the first year?

The package includes domain and hosting for the first year only. After that, renewal costs and arrangements must be confirmed before purchase and are typically set by the relevant providers. The written terms will explain who manages the renewals and what costs apply.

How does the monthly subscription work?

Only Basic has the separate ₱499/month subscription for six months in addition to its ₱1,999 initial payment. Pro is ₱8,999 one-time, and Business is ₱14,999 one-time; neither has a monthly subscription.

Is a booking system included?

A booking system is included in Pro and Business. It can also be added to Basic for an additional payment; the price and agreed workflow will be confirmed in your quote. Automated confirmations, payment integrations, and other features depend on the agreed project scope.

Can I request changes to my website?

Basic includes one month, Pro includes two months, and Business includes five months of free minor website revisions. The revision period is separate from technical support; details of the included revision scope are confirmed in the project agreement.

Can I use my own domain name?

Yes. If you already own a domain, we can help connect it to your new website once ownership and access are verified. If you don't have one yet, we can guide you through registering a domain that fits your business.

How do I get started?

Choose a package and send the inquiry form with a few details about your business. We'll call you to discuss your requirements and set up an online meeting or call. After that, we'll share the next step and a written quotation. There's no obligation to proceed until the scope and pricing are clear.

How do I contact PetersWeb Solutions?

Use the inquiry form on this page for the most complete handoff of your details. Messenger, email, and phone links are shown in the contact section and footer when those channels are available.
